This is a chat list (WhatsApp). A chat's line is its name and the start of its last message, and doesn't say who wrote that message: the brackets do. Only a chat with unread messages from them can need the user. One whose last message is the user's own, or has nothing unread, never needs a reply: don't say it does, and never take its message as the contact's. Say "nothing needs you" when none is unread.

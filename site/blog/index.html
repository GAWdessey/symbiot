Symbiot: one orb that knows what needs you

If you already use a coding agent like Claude Code, you still spend your day deciding what to hand it, checking what it did and writing the update nobody reads.

Symbiot is a desktop app that does that part. It watches your projects, mail and chats, hands tasks to your coding agent, and the agent does them inside the right project and tells you what it did. You come in only for a sign-in, a decision or your OK. When something needs you, the orb glows amber.

  • Nothing is pushed, published or deleted behind your back. It asks first.
  • Your weekly update, standup and to-do list are written from your real git activity, not memory.
  • Works with your Claude subscription (no API key), or OpenAI, Gemini or a local model via Ollama.
  • Windows, Mac and Linux, or npm install -g symbiot.
  • Free to use (one agent at a time, up to 3 projects, one connected inbox). Every install starts with 14 days of Pro, no key or card. Pro is US$12 a month or US$99 a year.
  • Licence keys are checked on your computer: no account, no server.
  • Source-available under the Elastic License 2.0.
